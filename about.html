ABOUT US

Built on trust, one bulk order at a time.

Prachaar Traders started with a simple idea: businesses that order promotional products deserve wholesale prices, real quality control and a partner who shows up on time — every time.

OUR STORY

Why we exist

Prachaar Traders was founded to close a gap we kept seeing in the market: businesses wanted branded products in bulk, but were stuck choosing between low-quality suppliers offering cheap prices, or premium vendors charging retail rates for wholesale volumes.

We built Prachaar Traders as a single, trusted supplier that could offer genuine wholesale pricing, consistent quality checks, and branding — logo, name or design — included as standard, not as a paid extra. Today we supply everything from packaged drinking water to corporate gift sets, for events, exhibitions and everyday office use.
